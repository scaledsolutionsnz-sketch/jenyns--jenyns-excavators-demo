Our services

Everything we take a machine to

Farm, civil and residential excavation across Ruawai, Dargaville, Matakohe, Paparoa, Maungaturoto and the wider Kaipara. No job is too big or too small.

Farm & rural

Work for farmers and rural blocks

The bread and butter. Getting water moving where it should, holding it where you want it, and building ground a herd can walk on in the wet.

01

Farm drains

New drains cut to grade, plus cleaning out and reshaping existing ones. Spoil spread back so the paddock stays workable.

02

Dams & effluent ponds

Water storage dams, stock ponds and effluent ponds shaped, keyed in and compacted to hold through a dry summer.

03

Races & feed pads

Farm races built to take a herd in the wet, plus feed pads, standoff pads and the metal to go with them.

04

Farm conversions & maintenance

Conversion earthworks, fence lines, tree clearing, culvert crossings and the general tidy-up work that never quite gets done.

05

Quarry ripping

Ripping and carting your own on-farm metal so you are not paying to truck rock in from town.

06

Drainage work

Subsoil drainage, outfalls and tile drains, laid out so the water actually gets away instead of ponding in the low corner.

Civil & council

Flood protection and infrastructure

Jenyns & Jenyns has years of experience on council and government work around Ruawai and the Kaipara flats, where stopbanks and floodgates do the heavy lifting.

07

Stopbanks

Construction, raising and repair of stopbanks, including the batters and tie-ins that decide whether a bank holds in a big rain.

08

Floodgates & culverts

Floodgate structures, culverts and crossings set, bedded and backfilled, with our own transporter bringing the gear and the pipe together.

09

Subdivisions

Bulk earthworks, roading formation, service trenching and stormwater for subdivisions in the Kaipara district.

Civil & residential

Sites, driveways and landscaping

For builders, developers and people putting a house or a shed on their own block.

10

House & shed sites

Site cuts, pads and foundation holes, set out and compacted so the builder can get straight into it.

11

Driveways

New driveways formed, metalled and shaped to drain, plus repairs to the ones that have gone soft in the wet.

12

Landscaping & tree clearing

Residential and commercial landscaping earthworks, tree and stump removal, and shaping ground into something you can actually use.
