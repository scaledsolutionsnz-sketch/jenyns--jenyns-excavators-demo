What we dig

Excavation work for farms, builders and councils

Most of what we do is dirt and water: getting water off a paddock, holding it where you want it, and building ground that stays put. If it needs a machine on it, ring us and we will tell you straight whether it is a job for us.

01

Farm drains & drain cleaning

New drains cut to grade and old ones cleaned out and reshaped, with the spoil spread back so you can still work the paddock afterwards.

02

Dams, ponds & effluent storage

Water storage dams, stock ponds and effluent ponds shaped, keyed in and compacted so they hold through a dry summer.

03

Stopbanks & flood protection

Stopbank construction and repair on the Kaipara flats, including the tie-ins and batters that decide whether a bank holds in a big rain.

04

Culverts, floodgates & pipework

Crossings, culverts and floodgate structures set, bedded and backfilled, with our own transporter bringing the gear and the pipe in together.

05

House sites, driveways & subdivisions

Site cuts, shed pads, driveways and subdivision earthworks for builders, developers and people putting a house on their own block.

06

Races, feed pads & farm conversions

Races that stand up to a herd in the wet, feed pads, fence lines, tree clearing and quarry ripping for on-farm metal.

About us

A Ruawai family that has been moving dirt here for over 40 years

Jenyns & Jenyns is run by Tye and Tim Jenyns out of Porritt Street in Ruawai. We work across Ruawai, Dargaville, Matakohe, Paparoa and the wider Kaipara for farmers, rural property owners, builders, developers, homeowners, councils and government departments.

We run our own excavators, trucks, tractors, rollers, trailers and transporters, so we are not waiting on anyone else to get a machine to your gate. No job is too big or too small, and you get a straight answer on price before we start.

  • Family owned and operating out of Ruawai since 1978
  • Our own fleet of excavators, trucks, tractors, rollers and transporters
  • Fully certified and insured, and trusted on council and government work
