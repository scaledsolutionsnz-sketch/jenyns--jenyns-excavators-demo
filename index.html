About us

Ruawai based, working the Kaipara

Jenyns & Jenyns Contracting runs out of Porritt Street in Ruawai. Tye and Tim take the calls, quote the work and run the machines, so the person who prices your job is the person who turns up to do it.

Most of the work is farms, lifestyle blocks and house sites around the Kaipara, plus council and government contracts in and around Ruawai. Forty years in one district means we know which ground drains and which ground does not.
