Privacy Policy

Placeholder wording for this demo. Jenyns & Jenyns should review and adjust it to match how they actually handle client information before the site goes live.

1. Who we are

Jenyns & Jenyns Excavators of 3 Porritt Street, Ruawai 0530, Northland operates this website. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need to quote and carry out work:

  • Your name, phone number and email address when you contact us.
  • The property address and details of the job you want done.
  • Site photographs, survey data and drone or LiDAR mapping taken as part of a job.
  • Billing details needed to invoice the work.

There is no contact form on this website. Any information you send comes to us because you chose to phone or email us directly.

3. How we use it

We use your information to prepare quotes, plan and carry out work, order materials, arrange cartage, invoice you, and keep in touch about your job. We may use site photographs in our own marketing, including this website and our social media, and will not identify a private property owner without asking first.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where it is needed to do the job, for example with subcontractors, suppliers, surveyors, engineers, our accountant, or a council or government client where the work requires it. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. Website analytics and cookies

This website may use standard analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. That data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally. Embedded content such as the Google Map may set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy.

6. Storage and security

Information is held on our own devices and in reputable cloud services, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, misuse or unauthorised access. We keep records for as long as we need them for the job and for the period required by New Zealand tax and business law.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Ring Tye on 021 292 1781 or email us through the contact section and we will sort it.

8. Complaints

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, talk to us first. If it is still not resolved, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version always sits on this page.
