Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026. Ruawai, Northland.

This is demo wording written for the website. Tye should read it over and adjust anything that does not match how the business works before the site goes live.

Jenyns & Jenyns Contracting handles personal information under the Privacy Act 2020. This page sets out what we collect, why, and what happens to it.

1. What we collect

Only what we need to quote and do the work:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • The property address and, where relevant, the legal description
  • Plans, consent documents and site photos you send us
  • Records of the work done, hours run and materials carted

There are no forms on this website. Nothing is collected while you browse. We only hold what you tell us when you ring, email, or meet us on site.

2. Why we hold it

To quote the job, order metal and materials, book cartage, invoice the work, and keep the records Inland Revenue requires.

3. Who sees it

Tye, Tim and anyone working on your job. Cartage contractors and quarries get what they need to deliver. Job and invoice records go to our accountant. Where a consent is involved, relevant details go to the Kaipara District Council or Northland Regional Council. We do not sell or trade client information.

4. Photos of finished work

We sometimes photograph completed jobs for the website or social media. We ask first, we do not publish the property address, and we take a photo down if you would rather it was not up.

5. Email and phone

The email buttons on this site open a message in your own email account. Nothing is sent through this website and nothing is stored on it. Emails and texts sit in the ordinary accounts we use for the business.

6. The map on this site

The contact section shows an embedded Google map of the Porritt Street yard. Loading it means Google may receive your IP address and set cookies under its own terms, which we do not control. Everything else on this site is served from our own pages.

7. How long we keep it

Job and invoice records are kept for seven years for Inland Revenue. Consent related records are kept as long as the councils require. Enquiries that never became a job are cleared out once they are no longer useful.

8. Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask what we hold about you and have anything wrong corrected. Call 021 292 1781 or email us and we will come back to you within 20 working days.

9. Complaints

Raise it with Tye first. If you are not satisfied, you can take it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

10. Contact
