FAQs

Questions people ask us

Straight answers, same as you’d get calling the yard.

What does NEXjen do?

NEXjen Group is a civil contractor based in Ruawai, Northland. We consult, design, construct and deliver infrastructure earthworks, including flood protection, land drainage, dams and reservoirs, stormwater and wastewater installation, and subdivisions.

How is NEXjen related to Jenyns & Jenyns?

Same family, same yard, same crew. Jenyns & Jenyns has done excavation and earthworks around the Kaipara since 1978. NEXjen is the civil and infrastructure side, adding design, project management and drone mapping.

Do you work for councils and government?

Yes. We deliver council and government-funded infrastructure work across Northland, including flood protection projects with the reporting that goes with them.

What is drone and LiDAR mapping used for?

It gives an accurate 3D model of the site before work starts, so we can work out cut-and-fill volumes, plan drainage and price the job on real numbers rather than guesses.

Can you design the job as well as build it?

Yes. We can take a project from concept and design through to construction and handover, so you deal with one team the whole way.

Do you build subdivisions?

Yes. We handle subdivision earthworks, including site preparation, stormwater and wastewater installation, and roading and access.

Where does NEXjen work?

We're based in Ruawai and work across the Kaipara District and Northland, including Dargaville, Maungaturoto and Whangārei.
