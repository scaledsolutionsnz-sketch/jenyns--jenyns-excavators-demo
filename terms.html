These terms cover the excavation and earthworks Jenyns & Jenyns Contracting ("we", "us") carries out for clients ("you").

1. Quotes

We quote on what we can see and what you tell us. A quote holds for 30 days. Rates assume reasonable access for the machine and float, and ground that can be worked in the conditions at the time.

2. Ground conditions

Kaipara ground varies. Rock, peat, springs, old fill and a high water table all change what a job takes. Where we hit conditions that could not reasonably be seen when quoting, we stop, show you, and agree the extra before carrying on.

3. Underground services

  • Before we dig you must tell us about private services on the property, including water lines, effluent and irrigation, power to sheds and pumps, and old drainage
  • Public utilities are located through the usual channels before work starts
  • We are not responsible for damage to private services that were not marked or disclosed

4. Consents

Earthworks, drainage and work near watercourses can need consent from the Kaipara District Council or Northland Regional Council. Unless we have agreed otherwise in writing, obtaining consent is your responsibility, and we will not start consentable work until it is in place.

5. Weather and timing

We give a realistic start date and keep you posted. Wet weather stops earthworks, and a wet week in the Kaipara can push a programme out. We will not work ground that will be damaged by working it.

6. Charging

Work is charged either at an agreed fixed price or at hourly machine and operator rates, whichever we set out in the quote. Float, cartage and materials such as metal are charged as quoted or at cost plus the agreed margin.

7. Payment

Invoices are due within seven days unless we have agreed account terms in writing. Longer jobs are invoiced in progress claims as the work reaches each agreed stage.

8. Our work

Tell us about any defect in our workmanship and we will come back and put it right. This sits alongside your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, which are not affected by anything here.

Where we form a surface to a specified level or fall, we warrant that shape at the time of handover. Settlement, stock damage, storm damage and erosion after we leave are not defects.

9. Health and safety

Live sites are hazardous. Please keep people, children and stock clear of the machines, and tell us about any hazard on the property before we start.
